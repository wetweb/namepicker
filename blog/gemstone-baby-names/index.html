Gemstone baby names

Updated 24 November 2026

Gemstone names are pretty, a little bit vintage, and come with built-in meaning. Many are birthstones, so you can pick one for the month your baby is due. Others mean “gem” or “pearl” in another language.

Tap any name to read its full meaning and origin.

Birthstone names

In month order, so you can find your due date.

  • Garnet (Girl or boy): January: the deep red gemstone.
  • Amethyst (Girl): February: the purple gemstone. Its Greek name means “not drunk”.
  • Diamond (Girl): April: from Greek for “unbreakable”.
  • Emerald (Girl): May: the green gemstone.
  • Pearl (Girl): June: the lustrous gem formed inside oysters.
  • Ruby (Girl): July: the red gemstone.
  • Sapphire (Girl): September: the blue gemstone.
  • Opal (Girl): October: the iridescent gemstone.
  • Topaz (Girl): November: the yellow gemstone.

More gemstone names

  • Beryl (Girl): From the pale green gemstone.
  • Jade (Girl or boy): From the green gemstone, once believed to cure kidney pain.
  • Amber (Girl): From the golden fossil resin and its colour.
  • Crystal (Girl): Sparkling glass or quartz, from the Greek word for “ice”.
  • Jacinth (Girl): From the orange gemstone, related to Hyacinth.
  • Opaline (Girl): A longer form of Opal, meaning “opal-like”.
  • Coral (Girl): From the reef-building sea creatures.

Names that mean gem or jewel

  • Gemma (Girl): Italian for “gem” or “jewel”. Dante’s wife was called Gemma.
  • Esmeralda (Girl): Spanish and Portuguese for “emerald”, the heroine of “The Hunchback of Notre-Dame”.
  • Jewel (Girl or boy): From the English word “jewel”.
  • Tiara (Girl): From the word for a jewelled crown.

Names that secretly mean pearl

Margaret comes from the Greek word for “pearl”, and so do its many short forms.

  • Margaret (Girl): From the Greek word for “pearl”, borne by many queens.
  • Greta (Girl): A short form of Margareta, borne by the actress Greta Garbo.
  • Maisie (Girl): A Scottish pet form of Mairead (Margaret).
  • Rita (Girl): A short form of Margherita. Rita Hayworth is a famous bearer.

Gemstone names for boys

  • Jasper (Boy): The name of a gemstone, and traditionally one of the three wise men.
  • Onyx (Girl or boy): From the black gemstone, from Greek for “nail” or “claw”.
  • Silver (Girl or boy): From the precious metal or the colour.
