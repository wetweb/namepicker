Flower names for girls

Updated 24 November 2026

Flower names have been loved since the Victorians, and they’re blooming again. This list starts with the classics, moves through the garden, and ends with names that mean “flower”, “rose” or “lily” in another language.

Tap any name to read its full meaning and origin.

Classic flower names

  • Rose (Girl): Originally a Germanic name meaning “famous kind”, but long associated with the rose.
  • Lily (Girl): From the white flower, a symbol of purity.
  • Daisy (Girl): From Old English for “day’s eye”. First used as a name in the 19th century.
  • Violet (Girl): From the purple flower, used as a name since the 19th century.
  • Poppy (Girl): From the bright red flower.
  • Iris (Girl): Greek for “rainbow”. Iris was the messenger of the gods, and the flower shares her name.
  • Jasmine (Girl): From the fragrant flower, from the Persian name Yasamin.

Garden flower names

  • Marigold (Girl): From the golden flower, whose name combines Mary and “gold”.
  • Primrose (Girl): From the primrose flower, from Latin for “first rose”.
  • Magnolia (Girl): From the flowering tree, named after the botanist Pierre Magnol.
  • Dahlia (Girl): From the flower, named after the Swedish botanist Anders Dahl.
  • Camellia (Girl): From the flowering shrub named after the botanist Georg Josef Kamel.
  • Peony (Girl): Named after Paeon, the physician of the Greek gods.
  • Azalea (Girl): From the azalea flower, from a Greek word meaning “dry”.
  • Zinnia (Girl): Named after the botanist Johann Zinn.
  • Lavender (Girl): From the fragrant flower or its pale purple colour.
  • Lilac (Girl): From the flowering shrub.
  • Hyacinth (Girl): From the hyacinth flower, or the gemstone of the same name.
  • Lotus (Girl): A symbol of purity in many cultures.
  • Calla (Girl): From the calla plant with white flowers, also linked with the Greek word for “beauty”.

Wildflower names

  • Heather (Girl): From the small flowering shrub of rocky moorland.
  • Clover (Girl): From the name of the wild flower.
  • Blossom (Girl): From the English word for a flower in bloom.
  • Posy (Girl): A pet form of Josephine, and also a small bunch of flowers.

Names that secretly mean flower

No petals in the spelling, but a flower hidden in the meaning.

  • Flora (Girl): From Latin “flos” (flower). Flora was the Roman goddess of blossoms and springtime.
  • Fleur (Girl): French for “flower”.
  • Rosa (Girl): Latin for “rose”. The civil rights activist Rosa Parks is a famous bearer.
  • Rhoda (Girl): From the Greek word for “rose”.
  • Rosalie (Girl): A form of Rosalia, from Latin “rosa” (rose).
  • Rosalind (Girl): Later linked to Latin “rosa linda” (beautiful rose). The heroine of “As You Like It”.
  • Susanna (Girl): From the Hebrew Shoshanna, meaning “lily”.
  • Lillian (Girl): Probably a pet form of Elizabeth, also linked with Latin “lilium” (lily).
  • Viola (Girl): Latin for “violet”, and the heroine of Shakespeare’s “Twelfth Night”.
  • Yasmin (Girl): From the Arabic and Hebrew word for “jasmine”.
