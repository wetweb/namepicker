Oliver

Oliver is a boy's name used in English, German, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish.

Meaning of Oliver

From the Old French Olivier, probably linked to the olive tree. Oliver is Roland's companion in "The Song of Roland", and Dickens' "Oliver Twist" made it famous.
