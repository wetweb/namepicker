Elmo

Elmo is a boy's name used in Italian, English, Finnish, Estonian.

Meaning of Elmo

Originally a short form of names ending in -helm (helmet), also linked to Saint Erasmus, the patron of sailors. Elmo of "Sesame Street" is a well-known bearer.
