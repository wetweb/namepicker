Alexander

Alexander is a boy's name used in English, German, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish.

Meaning of Alexander

From the Greek name Alexandros, meaning "defender of men". Its most famous bearer was Alexander the Great, the Macedonian king who built a vast empire in the 4th century BC.
