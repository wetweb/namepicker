Tatiana

Tatiana is a girl's name used in Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Romanian, French, Slovak.

Meaning of Tatiana

The feminine form of the Roman name Tatianus. Saint Tatiana was a 3rd-century martyr especially honoured in Russia.
