Linda

Linda is a girl's name used in English, German, Dutch, Italian, Swedish, Norwegian.

Meaning of Linda

Originally a short form of Germanic names with the element "lind" (soft, gentle). It also means "beautiful" in Spanish and was the most popular US girls' name around 1950.
