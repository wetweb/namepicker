Ariel

Ariel is a unisex name used in Hebrew.

Meaning of Ariel

From Hebrew, meaning "lion of God", and used in the Bible as a name for Jerusalem. Shakespeare gave it to a spirit in "The Tempest", and Disney's "The Little Mermaid" made it popular for girls.
